# Order Fulfillment Process Improvement  
## Business Analyst Case Study (Simulated)

---

## Overview
This project is a **simulated Business Analyst case study** created to demonstrate how I approach analyzing operational business problems, defining KPIs, and supporting decision-making in a retail order fulfillment context.

The scenario reflects common challenges faced by retail organizations with online ordering capabilities, including delayed order processing, inventory mismatches, and reactive issue handling.

> **Note:** This is a fictional case study created for portfolio and learning purposes. It does not represent a real organization or proprietary data.

---

## Business Context
A retail chain with both in-store and online sales is experiencing frequent order delays and inventory mismatches between online orders and warehouse availability. These issues have resulted in delayed shipments, order cancellations, and increased customer complaints.

Although operational issues are logged through a ticketing system, leadership lacks visibility into recurring patterns, root causes, and fulfillment performance trends.

---

## Problem Statement
The organization does not have centralized insight into where order fulfillment delays occur or how inventory mismatches impact delivery timelines. Issues are handled reactively through tickets, without trend analysis or KPI-based monitoring, limiting the ability to proactively improve operations.

## My Role
**Role:** Business Analyst

**Responsibilities**
- Analyze order fulfillment and issue-tracking data  
- Define KPIs to measure operational performance  
- Identify trends and root causes of delays and inventory mismatches  
- Translate findings into actionable business recommendations  

---

## Current State (AS-IS Process)
1. Customer places an online order  
2. Order is recorded in the order management system  
3. Inventory availability is validated manually or via delayed system sync  
4. Inventory mismatches or delays are discovered after order placement  
5. Issues are logged in a Jira-style ticketing system  
6. Customer support responds reactively to impacted customers  
7. Management reviews issues individually without trend analysis  

**Key Challenges**
- No KPI tracking for order fulfillment performance  
- Late detection of inventory mismatches  
- Reactive issue resolution driven by tickets  

---

## Future State (TO-BE Process)
1. Customer places an online order  
2. Inventory availability is validated before order confirmation  
3. Orders proceed to fulfillment only when inventory is confirmed  
4. Fulfillment events are tracked against defined KPIs  
5. Tickets are categorized using standardized issue types  
6. Operations reviews recurring issue trends during regular operational reviews  

**Improvements Introduced**
- Early validation instead of late detection  
- KPI-driven visibility into fulfillment performance  
- Proactive identification of recurring issues  

---

## Business Requirements
- BR-01: The business needs visibility into order fulfillment delays and inventory mismatches  
- BR-02: Operations requires standardized KPIs to measure fulfillment performance  
- BR-03: Issue data must be categorized to support trend analysis  
- BR-04: Stakeholders require summarized insights for operational decision-making  

---

## Data Sources
- Order records (order date, shipment date, fulfillment status)  
- Inventory availability reports  
- Ticketing system data (issue category, resolution time, priority)  

---

## KPIs Defined

| KPI | Purpose | Business Decision Enabled |
|---|---|---|
| Order Processing Time | Measure fulfillment efficiency | Identify process bottlenecks |
| % Orders Delayed Beyond SLA | Track service performance | Escalate high-risk periods |
| Inventory Mismatch Rate | Monitor inventory accuracy | Improve validation controls |
| Ticket Volume by Category | Identify recurring issues | Prioritize corrective actions |
| Avg Ticket Resolution Time | Measure operational workload | Improve resource planning |

---

## Analysis Performed
- Reviewed historical order and ticket data to identify delay patterns  
- Categorized tickets to determine recurring issue types  
- Analyzed the relationship between inventory mismatches and order delays  
- Identified high-impact periods with increased operational disruption  

---

## Key Insights
- Inventory mismatches were a primary contributor to delayed orders  
- Fulfillment delays were concentrated during high-volume periods  
- Certain issue categories consistently exceeded resolution SLAs  

---

## Recommendations
- Introduce KPI tracking for order fulfillment and inventory mismatches  
- Use ticket trend analysis to proactively identify recurring issues  
- Improve inventory validation before order confirmation  
- Provide summary dashboards to support operational reviews  

---

## Limitations & Next Steps
- The analysis was limited by the absence of real-time inventory data  
- Customer segmentation was not available  
- Future enhancements could include:
  - Real-time inventory integration  
  - Customer impact analysis by product category or region
